Privacy Policy

StatusNest

Last updated: 2026-08-31

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how StatusNest ("the App") handles information when you use it to save WhatsApp status photos and videos. StatusNest is a focused, WhatsApp-status-only app — it does not include Instagram or any other platform support — and runs entirely on your device, with no backend server or account system of its own.

Data We Collect

StatusNest only reads the WhatsApp status photos and videos that WhatsApp (or WhatsApp Business) already stores on your phone, and saves copies you choose to keep into your own device gallery. Nothing is uploaded, tracked, or sent anywhere.

Permissions We Use

  • Storage / media access — used only to find WhatsApp's status folder and to save the photos and videos you choose to download into your device gallery.

The App also checks whether WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business is installed, solely to power the optional "Send to WhatsApp" share button. No other app data is accessed. None of these permissions are used to transmit data off your device.

How We Use Information

Information accessed by the App is used solely to display and let you save WhatsApp status media that is already present on your device. The App does not use this information for advertising or profiling.

Data Sharing

The App does not sell, rent, or share your media or personal information with third parties, and does not integrate any analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. StatusNest is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to WhatsApp LLC.

Data Storage and Security

Saved statuses are stored only in your device's own gallery/media store. No method of electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by an updated "last updated" date in this document. Continued use of the App after changes are published constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

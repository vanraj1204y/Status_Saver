Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026

Data we collect

StatusNest runs entirely on your device. It only reads the WhatsApp status photos and videos that WhatsApp already stores on your phone, and saves copies you choose to keep into your own device gallery. Nothing is uploaded, tracked, or sent anywhere — the app has no server or account system of its own.

Permissions

StatusNest requests only the storage/media permissions needed to find WhatsApp's status folder and to save the photos and videos you download to your gallery.
